# High-dose vitamin C in people with cancer: a Cochrane-level systematic review and meta-analysis
Raphael Malikian (rtmalikian@gmail.com), Palmdale, CA, USA. ORCID 0009-0008-4326-4205.
Preprint: Zenodo, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24213677 — https://zenodo.org/records/24213677 (concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24213676). Version v1, CC BY 4.0.
Systematic Reviews Hub: https://rtmalikian.github.io/systematic-reviews/
Contents: manuscript PDF/DOCX/MD, analysis code, analysis-ready datasets, figures, search log, screening and extraction records, risk of bias files, peer review record.
Headline: 18 studies; IV RCT survival by cancer type: colorectal HR 1.04, pancreatic HR 0.46 (small, stopped early), NSCLC HR 0.33 (with hyperthermia), prostate HR 1.98. Low risk of bias trials pooled: HR 1.26 (0.71 to 2.24). Licence for the article: CC BY 4.0 (on deposit).
